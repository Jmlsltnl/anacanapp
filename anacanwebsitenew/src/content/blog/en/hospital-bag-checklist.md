Aim to have your hospital bag packed by week 35–36 — that way everything is ready even if labor starts unexpectedly. The most practical approach is three separate sections: mom's bag, baby's bag and the partner's bag. The checklist below covers an average 2–3 day hospital stay.

<div class="takeaways">
<h2>Key takeaways</h2>
<ul>
<li>Pack the bag by week 35–36</li>
<li>Keep documents (ID, maternity notes) in a separate folder</li>
<li>Bring baby clothes in different sizes — you can't know the weight in advance</li>
<li>Your partner needs their own mini-bag too</li>
<li>Install the car seat in advance — it's required for the ride home</li>
</ul>
</div>

## Documents first

- ID and health insurance documents
- Maternity notes / pregnancy record and latest ultrasound results
- Your birth plan, if you've agreed one with the hospital

## Mom's bag

**For labor:** a comfortable nightgown or birth shirt, warm socks, hair ties, lip balm (lips get very dry during labor), a water bottle and light snacks.

**For after birth:** 2–3 front-opening nightgowns (for breastfeeding), a robe and slippers, postpartum pads, a nursing bra and pads, toiletries, a phone charger (long cable!) and a going-home outfit — sized like your sixth month of pregnancy.

## Baby's bag

- 4–6 onesies and sleepsuits (sizes 50/56 and 62 — you won't know which fits)
- A hat, socks and scratch mittens
- Newborn diapers (size 1, a small pack is enough)
- Wet wipes and diaper cream
- A soft blanket and a season-appropriate going-home outfit
- A car seat — required for the ride home, installed in advance

## Partner's bag

Labor can take a long time — the partner should be prepared too: a change of clothes, snacks, water, a phone charger, small change (for vending machines and parking) and comfortable shoes. In Anacan's [Partner mode](/en/partner-mode/), your partner can see the shared bag checklist and tick off their own section.

## What gets forgotten most often?

1. A phone charger with a long cable
2. Lip balm
3. Your own pillow (hospital pillows are often thin)
4. Photocopies of documents
5. A comfortable going-home outfit for mom
6. A season-appropriate going-home outfit for baby
7. A small notebook for postpartum notes — or a log in the app
