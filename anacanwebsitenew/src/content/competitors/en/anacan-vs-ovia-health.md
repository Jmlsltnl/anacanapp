Quick answer: **Ovia Health** is actually three separate apps — Ovia Fertility, Ovia Pregnancy, and Ovia Parenting — best known in the US for deep pregnancy content and a strong presence inside employer health programs. **Anacan** covers the same journey, from cycle to toddlerhood, inside one continuous app, and adds a 24/7 AI assistant, a real partner mode, and native support for five languages. Here's how they compare.

## What is Ovia Health?

Ovia Health was founded in 2012 and is based in Boston, USA (oviahealth.com). Its distribution model is unusual: rather than app-store discovery, Ovia is distributed mainly through US employers and health insurance plans as part of workplace benefits, though its consumer apps are also available for direct download. Importantly, Ovia isn't one app — it's three: Ovia Fertility, Ovia Pregnancy, and Ovia Parenting, each built for a different life stage. Ovia Parenting is particularly known for strong week-by-week pregnancy content and detailed baby tracking.

## Where Anacan and Ovia are similar

- Both offer structured week-by-week pregnancy content
- Both include baby-tracking tools for feeding, sleep, and growth
- Both are available on iOS and Android
- Both emphasize science-based, expert-reviewed content

## What makes Anacan different

**One app, not three.** With Ovia, moving from fertility to pregnancy to parenting means switching apps — and often starting over. Anacan keeps the entire Flow → Bump → Mommy → Partner journey inside one account and one app, so your history carries over automatically as your stage changes.

**Native, not just translated.** Ovia's content and distribution are built primarily around the US and English-speaking users. Anacan is written natively in Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish, and Kazakh — not machine-translated on top of an English base.

**Anacan.AI.** A 24/7 AI assistant answers in five languages and adapts to whatever stage you're in. None of Ovia's three apps includes a comparable AI chat assistant.

**Local context.** Anacan's vaccine calendar is aligned with Azerbaijan's national schedule, and its maternity leave (dekret) calculator is based on local labor law — a detail global apps rarely get right for this region.

**Partner mode.** Anacan gives partners their own mode with SOS alerts, daily summaries, and an AI advisor. Public information about Ovia doesn't describe an equivalent dedicated partner experience.

## Ovia's strengths

To be fair, Ovia has real strengths. Its week-by-week pregnancy content is deep and has been refined over more than a decade, and being used by large US employers as part of workplace health benefits gives it a level of enterprise credibility that few consumer health apps can claim.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Anacan if:**
- You want one app and one account for cycle, pregnancy, baby care, and partner support
- Getting content natively in Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish, or Kazakh matters to you
- You want a 24/7 AI assistant that answers in your language
- Local context — a national vaccine calendar and a maternity leave calculator — is important to you
- You want your partner actively involved through a dedicated mode

**Ovia might be a better fit if:**
- You're in the US and your employer already offers Ovia as part of a benefits program
- You don't mind using a separate, specialized app for each life stage
- Deep, English-language editorial pregnancy content is your top priority

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Ovia Health free?** Its main distribution model runs through employers and insurance plans, so many users get free or discounted access that way; direct consumer pricing varies by app and isn't published as one figure. Anacan is free, with Premium at a fixed 7.99 ₼/month or 49.99 ₼/year.

**Can I use Ovia outside the US?** Its consumer apps can be downloaded worldwide, but the content and distribution model are built primarily around US, English-speaking users — there's no dedicated Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish, or Kazakh localization.

**Does Ovia have anything like Anacan's Partner mode?** Based on public information, none of the three Ovia apps describes a dedicated mode for dads or partners comparable to Anacan's.

**Do I have to install all three Ovia apps?** Yes, depending on your stage — Fertility, Pregnancy, and Parenting are distributed as separate apps. Anacan keeps Flow, Bump, Mommy, and Partner in one app, so there's nothing new to install when your stage changes.
