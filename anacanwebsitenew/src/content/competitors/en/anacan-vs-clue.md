Quick answer: **Clue** is one of the world's most scientifically credible period and cycle trackers, valued for its research partnerships and inclusive design. **Anacan** covers the same ground and then continues through pregnancy, your baby's first years and partner support — in 5 languages, including Azerbaijani, Turkish and Kazakh.

## What is Clue?

Clue is a cycle-tracking app from BioWink GmbH, founded in 2013 and based in Berlin, Germany. Clue built its brand on scientific rigor: it has collaborated with researchers at institutions including Oxford and Stanford on reproductive health, and positions itself as an inclusive tool for cisgender women, non-binary people and trans men alike. Core features are period prediction, ovulation estimates and a symptom log in a clean, minimalist interface. A free tier covers the basics, while "Clue Plus" unlocks deeper insights and an ad-free experience. Clue does include some pregnancy tracking, but its identity stays rooted in the cycle, not a full pregnancy-to-baby journey.

## Where Anacan and Clue are similar

- Both offer a period calendar, ovulation predictions and a detailed symptom/mood diary
- Both work on a free-core-plus-paid-subscription model
- Both include some pregnancy tracking alongside the cycle
- Both favor a clean, uncluttered interface

## What makes Anacan different

**A full four-stage journey, not just the cycle.** Clue's strength is the menstrual cycle; once you're pregnant, or want to track your baby's growth or vaccines, you'll typically need a separate app. Anacan keeps you in one place through Flow (cycle) → Bump (pregnancy) → Mommy (baby) → Partner, so your history carries forward instead of starting over.

**Anacan.AI, a 24/7 assistant.** Anacan's built-in AI assistant understands your stage — cycle day, pregnancy week or your baby's age — and answers in your own language at any hour. Clue has no comparable AI chat assistant.

**A real partner mode.** Anacan has a dedicated Partner experience, so dads (or any support person) get their own updates, SOS alerts and daily summaries. Clue is built around the individual user, with no equivalent.

**Native localization, including Azerbaijani, Turkish and Kazakh.** Anacan is built natively in 5 languages — not machine-translated add-ons. Clue is strong in major European languages but doesn't offer the same depth for Azerbaijani, Turkish, Kazakh or Russian speakers.

**Local context for Azerbaijan.** Anacan's vaccine calendar follows Azerbaijan's national immunization schedule, and its maternity-leave calculator is based on local legislation — details a globally focused app like Clue rarely addresses.

## Clue's strengths

It's worth being fair: Clue has earned its reputation. Collaborations with researchers at universities such as Oxford give it a level of scientific credibility hard to match, and its commitment to inclusive design — welcoming non-binary and trans users, not only cisgender women — is genuinely progressive. Its interface is famously clean and minimalist, and its brand carries real trust across Europe.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Anacan if:**
- You want one app that follows you from cycle tracking through pregnancy and your baby's early years
- You'd like a 24/7 AI assistant that understands your exact stage
- You want your partner genuinely involved through a dedicated mode of his own
- You want content and calculators built around Azerbaijan or the wider Azerbaijani-, Turkish-, Kazakh- and Russian-speaking world

**Clue might be a better fit if:**
- You want cycle tracking shaped by academic research partnerships
- Inclusive design for non-binary or trans users is a top priority
- You don't need pregnancy or baby tools and prefer a narrowly focused app

## Frequently asked questions

**Will I lose my data if I switch from Clue to Anacan (or the other way around)?** Each app stores data in its own system, with no direct transfer between them — though Anacan lets you export your logs as a PDF report.

**Can I use both apps at once?** Technically yes, but logging the same symptoms twice wastes time for little benefit — most people settle on one that fits their current stage of life.

**Does Clue support pregnancy and baby tracking the way Anacan does?** Clue includes some pregnancy features, but it wasn't built for a full pregnancy-to-baby journey, and has no tool for tracking growth, vaccines or sleep from birth to age 5 like Anacan's Mommy mode.

**Which app is better for non-English speakers in Azerbaijan or Central Asia?** Anacan is built natively for Azerbaijani, Turkish, Kazakh and Russian speakers, with local content like Azerbaijan's vaccine calendar. Clue's localization is strongest in major European languages.
