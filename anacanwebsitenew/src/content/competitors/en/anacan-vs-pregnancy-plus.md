Quick answer: **Pregnancy+** is a beautifully designed, pregnancy-only app best known for its 3D fetal visuals — but once your baby arrives, it hands you off to a separate app, and it never tracked your cycle. **Anacan** follows you from your period through pregnancy to your child's 5th birthday in one continuous app, with a real partner mode and a 24/7 AI assistant built in.

## What is Pregnancy+?

Pregnancy+ is made by Health & Parenting Ltd, headquartered in Malta, and ranks among the most downloaded, highest-rated pregnancy apps worldwide. Its signature feature is a gorgeous 3D visualization of your baby's development, alongside core tools like a week-by-week guide, kick counter, contraction timer and symptom tracker. It's free to download, with a premium subscription unlocking extra content. One important detail: Pregnancy+ is built for pregnancy only — once you give birth, the same maker points you to a separate app, Baby+ Daily Care, to keep tracking your child.

## Where Anacan and Pregnancy+ are similar

- Both include a kick counter to log your baby's movements
- Both offer a contraction timer to help you time labor
- Both offer detailed week-by-week fetal development content
- Both use a free app + optional premium subscription model

## What makes Anacan different

**One journey, not three apps.** Pregnancy+ covers only the 40 weeks of pregnancy. Anacan's Flow → Bump → Mommy → Partner path starts with your menstrual cycle, continues through pregnancy, and runs to your child's 5th year — in the same app, account and logs.

**No app-switching after birth.** With Pregnancy+, delivery day means starting over in a new app, Baby+ Daily Care. In Anacan, Mommy mode simply picks up where Bump left off — same login, same history, nothing to re-enter.

**Cycle tracking from day one.** Pregnancy+ has no period or ovulation tracking at all, so it can't help you before a pregnancy or between pregnancies. Anacan's Flow mode covers exactly that, from the start.

**A genuine partner mode.** Anacan's Partner mode brings dads into daily updates, alerts and milestones — a level of involvement Pregnancy+ simply doesn't offer.

**Anacan.AI.** Anacan's built-in AI assistant understands your exact stage — cycle day, pregnancy week or your baby's age — and answers in 5 languages, 24/7. Pregnancy+ has little to no conversational AI support.

**Local Azerbaijan content.** Anacan's vaccine calendar follows Azerbaijan's national schedule, and its maternity-leave calculator is based on local law — the kind of local detail a global app like Pregnancy+ simply doesn't provide.

## Pregnancy+'s strengths

Credit where it's due: Pregnancy+ has some of the most polished 3D fetal visuals on the market, and its language localization is broad and well executed — a real strength for a worldwide audience. Its pregnancy toolset also feels thoughtfully built-in, with the kick counter and contraction timer sitting naturally alongside the weekly content.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Anacan if:**
- You want cycle, pregnancy and your baby's first 5 years in one continuous app
- You don't want to switch apps or lose your history the day you give birth
- You want your partner genuinely involved through a dedicated mode
- You want a 24/7 AI assistant that answers in your own language
- You care about tools built for Azerbaijan, like the vaccine calendar and maternity-leave calculator

**Pregnancy+ might be a better fit if:**
- You want the most visually polished 3D pregnancy experience on the market
- You only need pregnancy tracking right now and don't mind switching apps after birth
- You need an interface language that Anacan doesn't support yet

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I track my period with Pregnancy+?** No. Pregnancy+ is built around pregnancy only and has no cycle or ovulation tracking — for that you'd need a separate app, such as Anacan's Flow mode.

**What happens after I give birth — can I keep using Pregnancy+?** Pregnancy+ itself doesn't track your baby's growth after delivery — its maker offers a separate app, Baby+ Daily Care, so you'd start fresh with a new history.

**Does Pregnancy+ have an AI assistant like Anacan.AI?** Not really. Pregnancy+ focuses on structured content — a development calendar, counters and trackers — rather than a conversational AI answering personal questions the way Anacan.AI does.

**Which app supports more languages?** Pregnancy+ genuinely supports a wide range of languages worldwide. Anacan focuses on doing 5 languages — Azerbaijani, English, Russian, Turkish and Kazakh — really well, with fully native content instead of machine translation.
