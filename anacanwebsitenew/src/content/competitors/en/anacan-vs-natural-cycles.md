Quick answer: **Natural Cycles** and **Anacan** solve different problems. Natural Cycles is a clinically validated, medical-device-grade method for avoiding or planning pregnancy through daily basal body temperature tracking. **Anacan** is a free superapp that follows you from your first cycle log through pregnancy, your baby's early years, and partner involvement, with a 24/7 AI assistant in 5 languages.

## What is Natural Cycles?

Natural Cycles is a Swedish app, founded in 2013 and headquartered in Stockholm — one of the first apps to receive FDA clearance as a digital contraception method, built around basal body temperature (BBT). Each morning you log your temperature (a compatible connected thermometer gives full accuracy, though a regular one works with less precision), and the app marks the day "red" or "green." You can switch between "Plan" mode to conceive and "Prevent" mode to avoid pregnancy without hormones, running on a subscription plus the cost of the recommended thermometer.

## Where Anacan and Natural Cycles are similar

- Both track your menstrual cycle and estimate your fertile window and ovulation day
- Both get more accurate the more consistently you log daily data
- Both use a freemium model with a paid subscription tier
- Both believe understanding your cycle should be simple, not stressful

## What makes Anacan different

**A full journey, not just a cycle.** Natural Cycles' scope ends at conception. Anacan continues: Flow (cycle) → Bump (week-by-week pregnancy) → Mommy (baby growth, vaccinations, sleep and feeding logs) → Partner (dad mode) — one app, one account.

**Anacan.AI.** A 24/7 AI assistant that understands your stage — cycle day, pregnancy week, or baby's age — and answers in your language. Natural Cycles has no conversational assistant.

**Five languages, real localization.** Anacan is built natively in Azerbaijani, English, Russian, Turkish, and Kazakh, adapted to each market rather than simply translated. Natural Cycles isn't deeply localized for these languages or regions.

**Local Azerbaijan context.** The vaccination calendar follows Azerbaijan's national schedule, and the maternity-leave calculator uses local legislation — detail most global apps skip.

**No dedicated hardware needed.** Anacan works with just your phone; Natural Cycles' accuracy depends on daily temperature discipline and, ideally, a specific thermometer.

## Natural Cycles' strengths

To be fair, Natural Cycles sits in a genuinely different, important category. Its FDA clearance and clinical validation as a contraceptive method are real credentials, not marketing — and they matter for anyone who needs a reliable, non-hormonal birth control method and is willing to log their temperature daily.

This is worth stating plainly: **Anacan's Flow mode is not a medical device and is not a substitute for contraception.** Its ovulation and fertile-window estimates support awareness and planning, not pregnancy prevention.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Anacan if:**
- One app that follows you from cycle tracking through pregnancy, your baby's first years, and partner involvement
- A native experience in Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish, or Kazakh, with local content like vaccination and maternity-leave calculators
- A 24/7 AI assistant that adjusts to your current stage
- You're tracking your cycle for general awareness, not relying on any single app as birth control

**Natural Cycles might be a better fit if you specifically need a clinically validated contraception method:**
- An FDA-cleared, non-hormonal way to avoid pregnancy, taking your temperature every day
- Your needs stop at cycle tracking and conception — no pregnancy, baby, or partner tracking required
- Using (or buying) a compatible basal body thermometer for full accuracy

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use Anacan instead of Natural Cycles for birth control?** No. Anacan's ovulation and fertile-window predictions are informational only, not a clinically validated contraceptive method. If you need contraception, talk to a doctor or use a validated method like Natural Cycles.

**Do I need a special thermometer for Natural Cycles?** For full accuracy, yes — a compatible basal body thermometer is recommended; a regular one works too, with less precision.

**Can I use Anacan and Natural Cycles together?** Technically yes, though double data entry takes extra effort — most people pick one based on their main need: contraception or full-journey tracking.

**Does Anacan track pregnancy and my baby the way Natural Cycles does?** No — Natural Cycles' scope ends around conception; Anacan continues with week-by-week pregnancy tracking, baby growth and vaccinations, and a partner mode.
