Quick answer: **What to Expect** is the app companion to one of the world's best-known pregnancy book franchises, with a huge, highly active community of expecting parents. **Anacan** offers the same week-by-week pregnancy tracking, plus the full journey from cycle to baby care and partner support — all in one app, in 5 languages, backed by the 24/7 Anacan.AI assistant. Here's a closer look at both.

## What is What to Expect?

What to Expect is the mobile app built around Heidi Murkoff's best-selling book franchise, "What to Expect When You're Expecting." Published by Everyday Health / Ziff Davis at whattoexpect.com, it carries exceptionally strong brand recognition thanks to its book heritage, especially in the US. Its core focus is week-by-week pregnancy content, paired with large, active community forums where women due the same month connect in dedicated "groups" — a feature many users genuinely love.

## Where Anacan and What to Expect are similar

- Both track pregnancy week by week with stage-appropriate content
- Both include a community feature where users share experiences and ask questions
- Both are free to use with ads, and each offers an optional premium tier that removes ads and unlocks extras
- Both surface daily symptom and development information throughout pregnancy

## What makes Anacan different

**The full motherhood journey, one app.** What to Expect focuses primarily on pregnancy; it doesn't go deep on tracking a baby's 0–5 growth stage with WHO growth charts and a vaccine calendar. Anacan brings the whole Flow → Bump → Mommy → Partner journey together in a single app.

**Real localization, not just translation.** What to Expect's content and community skew heavily US-centric and English-first, with no equivalent depth for Azerbaijani, Turkish, Kazakh or Russian speakers. Anacan is built natively in all five languages from day one.

**Anacan.AI.** Our 24/7 AI assistant adapts to your exact stage — cycle day, pregnancy week or your baby's age — and answers in your own language. What to Expect has no built-in equivalent.

**Local Azerbaijani context.** Anacan's vaccine calendar follows Azerbaijan's national schedule, and its maternity-benefit calculator is based on local regulations — details a US-centric global app doesn't cover.

**A dedicated partner mode.** Anacan's Partner mode lets dads follow along actively with SOS alerts, daily summaries and an AI advisor of their own. What to Expect offers no equivalent.

## What to Expect's strengths

Let's be fair: What to Expect has real strengths of its own. Decades of trust built through a best-selling book franchise give it a level of brand credibility that's hard to match. Its community forums — where parents due the same month gather in "groups" — are large, long-established and remarkably active.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Anacan if:**
- You want native content and AI support in Azerbaijani, Turkish, Kazakh or Russian
- You'd rather stay in one app from cycle tracking through motherhood, baby growth and vaccines
- You want your partner actively involved, with SOS alerts when it matters most
- A national vaccine calendar and maternity calculator matter to you

**What to Expect might be a better fit if:**
- You want English pregnancy content built on a trusted, decades-old book franchise
- You're looking for a large, US-centric community of other expecting parents
- You only need pregnancy tracking, not baby-stage or partner tools

## Frequently asked questions

**Will I lose my data if I switch from Anacan to What to Expect (or vice versa)?** Each app stores data in its own system, and there's no direct transfer — but Anacan lets you export your logs as a PDF report.

**Can I use both apps at the same time?** Technically, yes — some people use What to Expect for its community and Anacan for full tracking and local content. Just note that logging everything twice takes extra time.

**Is What to Expect's community bigger than Anacan's?** Its US-centric forums, built over many years around the book franchise, are large and well established. Anacan's community is newer but growing quickly across Azerbaijan and the wider Turkic- and Russian-speaking world.

**Which app is better for partners?** Anacan has a dedicated Partner mode with SOS alerts, daily summaries and its own AI advisor for dads. What to Expect offers no equivalent partner experience.
